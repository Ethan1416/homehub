RepLog — Privacy Policy

Effective June 3, 2026

What we collect

• Account info: your email and display name.
• App data you enter: workouts, sets/reps/weights, meals and checklist items, notes, and calendar events.
• Connected health data: if you connect an Oura ring, readiness, sleep, activity, heart-rate variability, resting heart rate, steps, and body-temperature metrics.
• Technical: basic data needed to keep you signed in and the app running.

How we use it

Only to provide the app to you — to store your logs, show your trends, estimate calories, and compute your rankings. We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

Where it lives

Your data is stored with our database/hosting provider (Supabase). Calorie estimates use public nutrition data from the USDA FoodData Central database. Health data is fetched from Oura on your behalf using a token you provide.

Your data is private to you

Your account’s gym, meal, and vitals data is associated with your account and is not shown to other users.

Your choices

You can edit or delete your entries in the app, disconnect Oura by revoking its token, and request deletion of your account and associated data.

Children

RepLog is not directed to children under 16 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Not medical advice

RepLog is a fitness, nutrition, and wellness tracking tool for general informational purposes only. It is not a substitute for professional medical advice.

Changes & contact

We’ll update this policy as the app evolves. For privacy requests, contact us at ethangrucza@gmail.com.
